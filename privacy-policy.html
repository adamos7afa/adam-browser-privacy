Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026 · App: ADAM BROWSER (com.adamapps.adam_browser) · Developer: Adam Apps · Contact: mostafalaa.9226@gmail.com

1. Summary

  • ADAM BROWSER has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no server operated by us.
  • We, the developer, do not receive or store your browsing history, bookmarks, cookies, downloads or any other personal data.
  • The data the app keeps (history, bookmarks, tabs, settings, cookies and similar website data) is stored on your device only.
  • When you open websites, search, or download files, the websites, search engines and servers involved receive the requests any web browser sends. They are independent of us and have their own policies.

3. Private tabs

Private tabs use a separate WebView profile. Their cookies, local storage and cache are kept apart from normal tabs, are not shared with them, and are removed after the private session ends (as soon as Android allows, and at the latest the next time the app starts). Pages and searches in private tabs are not added to history, form data and passwords are not saved, and private tabs are not restored after a restart.

Limits you should know about: files downloaded from a private tab stay in your Downloads folder and may appear in the downloads list; websites, your network operator and your internet provider can still see your activity. If the WebView on a device does not support separate profiles, private tabs are disabled instead of being offered without isolation.

4. What leaves your device

  • Websites you visit. Loading a page sends a request to that site (including your IP address and any cookies that site set). What the site does with it is governed by its own privacy policy.
  • Your chosen search engine. When you search from the address bar, the text you type is sent to the engine you selected in Settings: Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing, Brave Search or Ecosia. Each has its own privacy policy.
  • Downloads. Files are fetched by the Android system download manager. For a download started from a page, the app passes that page's cookies, the browser user-agent and the page address as referrer to the download request so the server can authorise it.
  • Google Safe Browsing. The app leaves the Android WebView's Safe Browsing protection switched on. This platform component, provided by Google, may check addresses of pages you open against Google's list of unsafe sites, and is governed by Google's policies.
  • Files you choose to upload. If a website offers a file upload, you pick the file with the Android file picker; the file is sent to that website only when you submit it. The app does not read your other files.
  • Opening other apps. If a page tries to open another app (for example a mail or phone link), the app asks first and does nothing unless you confirm.
  • The ad and tracker blocker works entirely on your device: it compares the host name of each request with a list built into the app, and sends nothing anywhere.

5. Permissions

  • Internet and network state — to load web pages.
  • Write to external storage (Android 9 and older only) — to save downloads to the Downloads folder. Android 10 and newer do not need this permission.

The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, photos, SMS or phone permissions. If a website asks to use location, camera, microphone or similar features, the request is denied.

6. Security

The app loads web pages over HTTPS and blocks plain-HTTP pages in release builds. Pages with an invalid, expired or untrusted certificate are blocked and cannot be opened by overriding the warning. No method of storing or transmitting data is perfectly secure, and the app relies on the security of the Android system and WebView on your device.

7. Children

ADAM BROWSER is a general-purpose browser and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, because the app does not send personal information to us. The web content you can open with any browser is outside our control.

8. Your choices and rights

Because we do not hold your data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You control the data on your device: use Settings → Clear browsing data, delete individual history entries, bookmarks and downloads, switch history and tab restore off in Privacy settings, use private tabs, or uninstall the app to remove all app data.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this page and the effective date above. The latest version is always available at this address.
